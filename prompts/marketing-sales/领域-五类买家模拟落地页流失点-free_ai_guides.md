## 复制这条 Prompt

把你的输入和下面这段 Prompt（源帖原文）放进同一条消息，再发送。

```text
"You are a buyer simulation panel. I'll paste a piece of copy (a landing page, sales page, email, or ad). Simulate five buyers reading it from top to bottom.

The five buyers:
1. The Ready Buyer. Has the problem, has the budget, wants to buy now. If they hesitate, something core is broken.
2. The Skeptic. Burned before. Trusts nothing without proof like numbers, case studies, or guarantees.
3. The Price Checker. Wants the outcome but weighs value against cost the whole way through.
4. The Confused Visitor. Can't tell what this is, how it works, or whether it's for them.
5. The Comparison Shopper. Weighing two or three alternatives and asking "why this and not that?"

For each buyer:
- Read the copy one section at a time.
- At each section, narrate their honest inner reaction in one or two lines.
- Mark the exact line where they decide to buy, keep reading, or leave.
- End with their verdict of BUY, ON THE FENCE, or BOUNCE, and the one reason behind it.

Then give me a synthesis:
- A friction map showing which sections lost the most buyers, flagging any section that lost three or more.
- The top three fixes in priority order, each with a specific rewrite I can paste straight in.

Be blunt. Give me the reactions a real buyer would think but never say to my face."
```
