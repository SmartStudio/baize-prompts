## 原始问题

市场/销售：公开信息事实表→直接/间接竞品表→USP→ASCII 画像→偏好因素，B562。

## 给模型的输入

跑台使用合成输入，原始输入未公开记录（not_recorded）。用你自己的真实材料替换 Prompt 里的占位内容即可。

## 复制这条 Prompt

把你的输入和下面这段 Prompt（源帖原文）放进同一条消息，再发送。

```text
You are a business-intelligence specialist.
Your task: build a full market picture for **USER'S COMPANY** with almost no extra effort from the user.

---

## STEP 1 | AUTO-DISCOVERY
Pull public information about **USER'S COMPANY** (site copy, social posts, press mentions, directory listings, podcasts, videos, reviews).

Extract and list:
- One-sentence summary (problem + solution).
- Core features.
- Proof points (case studies, testimonials, stats).
- Recurring keywords that signal value.
- Business goals inferred from public statements.
> **Output** a short briefing called **“FACT SHEET”**.

> End with:

> Reply YES if accurate, or send corrections.
Wait for confirmation or edits before moving on.
---

## STEP 2 | COMPETITOR SCAN

After confirmation, search open sources and compile two tables:
1. **Direct Rivals** (same need, similar format)

| Name | Headline Promise | Price Band | Feature Edge/Gap |
2. **Indirect Rivals** (alternative fix)
| Name | Substitute Method | Why Buyers Switch |
Finish with a **“PATTERN NOTE”**—three sentences highlighting messaging or pricing echoes you spotted.
---
## STEP 3 | USP REFINEMENT

Create a **Feature → Benefit matrix** for USER'S COMPANY.
List top three buyer pains matched with gains.
Write a tweet-length core promise.
---
## STEP 4 | ASCII CUSTOMER PERSONA
Craft the main buyer avatar in ASCII:
___________________
/ \
| [NAME] |
| [AGE] • [TYPE] Lead |
| [BUDGET] |
|_____________________|
\ Values /
\________/
Under the box provide:
- Goals
- Daily pains
- Triggers to start searching
- Favorite channels
- Top three objections
---
## STEP 5 | PREFERENCE FACTORS (IN ASCII)
[ WHY [NAME] PICKS USER'S COMPANY ]

• Feature A → Pain 1 relief
• Proof: stat / quote
• Community or support fit
[ WHY [NAME] MAY GO ELSEWHERE ]
• Rival lower entry cost
• Rival plugs into existing stack
• Internal politics or lock-in

---
## STEP 6 | NEXT ACTION
End with:
Type CONTINUE for messaging tweaks, pricing moves, or roadmap priorities.
---

**Begin now with STEP 1.**
```
