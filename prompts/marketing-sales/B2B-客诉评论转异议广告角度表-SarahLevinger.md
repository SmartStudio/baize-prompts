## 原始问题

一般 B2B 获客/客服补位：输入客诉/工单 → 三列表（原话/底层异议/新广告角度），格式可核对。

## 给模型的输入

跑台使用合成输入，原始输入未公开记录（not_recorded）。用你自己的真实材料替换 Prompt 里的占位内容即可。

## 复制这条 Prompt

把你的输入和下面这段 Prompt（源帖原文）放进同一条消息，再发送。

```text
Role: You are a Performance Creative Director.

Task: Analyze the messy, petty customer comments below and turn their complaints into brand-new ad concepts designed to handle objections upfront.

Raw Customer Comments:
[Paste ad comments, support tickets, or survey responses here]

Rules:

1. Filter out the noise: Ignore generic trolls. Focus on complaints that reveal real doubts, price sensitivity, or confusion.

2. Flip the narrative: Turn every valid complaint into a creative hook or visual concept that addresses the fear head-on before the viewer can complain. Use reverse psychology or humor as necessary.

3. Output Format: Create a table with 3 columns:

Raw Customer Complaint⁠

The Underlying Objection⁠

New Ad Angle / Hook Idea⁠
```
