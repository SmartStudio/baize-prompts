## 原始问题

求职全流程 12 条短提示（薪资谈判、STAR 故事、90 天计划、异议预处理等），各条可独立跑。

## 复制这条 Prompt

把你的输入和下面这段 Prompt（源帖原文）放进同一条消息，再发送。

```text
1. Salary Negotiation Script
Navigate compensation like a pro.
PROMPT:
“I’m interviewing for [role] at [company]. Market rate is $[X]. Generate 3 counter-offer scenarios with supporting data points and negotiation phrases that maintain relationship while maximizing value.”

2. Achievement Story Bank
Transform experience into compelling narratives.
PROMPT:
“Convert these accomplishments [list 3–5] into STAR stories. Emphasize strategic thinking, cross-functional leadership, and measurable business impact. Keep each under 2 minutes.”

3. Power Question Generator
Ask questions that position you as a peer.
PROMPT:
“Create 5 strategic questions for [role/company] that demonstrate business acumen, industry knowledge, and executive thinking. Include questions about growth challenges and competitive positioning.”

4. 90-Day Plan Creator
Show you’re ready to deliver immediately.
PROMPT:
“Design a 30-60-90 day plan for [role] focusing on quick wins, stakeholder alignment, and strategic initiatives. Format for 5-minute presentation.”

5. Executive Summary Builder
Craft a compelling opener that commands attention.
PROMPT:
“Here’s my background: [paste resume]. Create a 90-second executive summary highlighting my top 3 achievements with quantifiable results. Focus on leadership impact and P&L responsibility.”

6. Interview Weakness Reframe
Turn vulnerabilities into strengths.
PROMPT:
“My potential weakness is [X]. Create an authentic response that shows self-awareness, growth mindset, and proactive improvement. Include specific actions I’ve taken.”

7. LinkedIn Outreach Engine
Connect with decision-makers directly.
PROMPT:
“Write 3 LinkedIn messages to [hiring manager/recruiter] about [role]. Vary approaches: mutual connection, industry insight, and value proposition. Keep under 150 words each.”

8. Company Research Deep Dive
Go beyond surface-level prep.
PROMPT:
“Analyze [company]’s last 3 earnings calls and news. Identify 3 strategic priorities and create talking points showing how my experience in [area] addresses their challenges.”

9. Executive Presence Optimizer
Communicate with C-suite confidence.
PROMPT:
“Transform this explanation of my work [paste description] into executive-level language. Use metrics, strategic impact, and business outcomes. Remove tactical details.”

10. Cultural Fit Demonstrator
Align with organizational values authentically.
PROMPT:
“[Company] values [list values]. Generate examples from my experience that demonstrate each value in action. Focus on leadership decisions and team impact.”

11. Follow-Up Accelerator
Stay top-of-mind strategically.
PROMPT:
“Draft a post-interview thank you that references [specific discussion points]. Include one new insight about their business and reiterate my unique value proposition.”

12. Objection Handler Toolkit
Address concerns before they’re voiced.
PROMPT:
“Common objections for my candidacy might be [list 2–3]. Create preemptive responses that reframe each as an advantage. Include proof points.”
```
