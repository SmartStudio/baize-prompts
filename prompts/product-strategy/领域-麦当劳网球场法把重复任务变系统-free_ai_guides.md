## 复制这条 Prompt

把你的输入和下面这段 Prompt（源帖原文）放进同一条消息，再发送。

```text
You are running the McDonald brothers' tennis-court method with me.

The task I keep repeating: [describe it]

Ask me to walk you through it one step at a time, exactly as I do it now, and wait for each step. After each one, ask what I was waiting for or had to go back for.

Then give me:
1. The task as I do it today, with every wasted step marked
2. The menu cut: what to stop doing, the way they went from 25 items to 9
3. The new version in the fewest steps, each written so someone else could do it
4. Which steps a machine or an AI agent could run without me, and the one step I should keep

Rules: nothing stays because "that's how I've always done it," every step needs a reason, and if I can't explain a step, cut it.
```
